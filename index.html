Hi, I'm Zoe, and I'm
Medical & Scientific Writer

I translate complex biomedical and clinical evidence into clear, compliant content for healthcare professionals, patients, and regulators. With an MSc in Biochemistry and 8+ years across pharma, medtech, and science communication, I bridge the gap between data and understanding.

What I do

• Regulatory writing (Clinical Study Reports, Investigator's Brochures, Clinical Protocols per FDA/EMA guidelines)
• Medical device & diagnostic content (clinical evidence summaries, HCP materials, patient-facing documents)
• Pharmaceutical writing (literature reviews, publication support, plain-language summaries)
• Visual science communication (infographics, slide decks, data visualisation for medical audiences)
